Flagship  ·  Stamps Scholar Research

Neural Repair Driven by Planarian Stem Cells (Neoblasts) across Neurodegenerative Cell and Animal Models

Independent Research  ·  Aug. 2020 – Present

Pioneering a computational therapeutics initiative aimed at dismantling the clinical standard of "managed decline" in neurodegenerative care. Originating as an independent exploration into planarian flatworm regeneration transplanted into earthworms and now advanced through funded Dartmouth Stamps Scholar research, the project leverages artificial intelligence to decode the biological mechanisms behind complete central nervous system regeneration. By translating these computational blueprints into targeted interventions for mammalian tissue, previous models demonstrated over 90% cell viability restoration in neurodegenerated pathways, laying the translational groundwork to transform conditions like Parkinson’s and Multiple Sclerosis from irreversible diagnoses into treatable biological challenges for mammalian wet-lab work.
